## Description

bingd helps you keep track of the shows you are watching, planning to watch, and waiting to return.

Build your library, follow friends, discover what is coming up, and get alerts when shows on your list have new episodes or returning seasons. It is designed for the simple habit that matters most: knowing what to watch and when it airs.

Features:

- Track shows across your personal library.
- See upcoming episodes for shows you follow.
- Get push notifications for shows airing tonight or returning soon.
- Follow friends and browse public profiles.
- Manage notification preferences by alert type.
- Use the same account across web and mobile.

bingd is built for people who love TV but do not want to keep release dates in their head.

## Review Notes Draft

bingd requires sign-in to create a watchlist and manage notification preferences. Testers can sign in, add shows from search, enable notifications from Settings, and verify that account deletion is available in Settings.

Push notifications are used only for user-selected show alerts.
